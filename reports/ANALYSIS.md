# Energy Brief — measured example

Research snapshot through 2024, not a live forecast. Compare observed endpoints and inspect missingness before interpreting trends.

## Results

- **Latest generation:** 2,036.5 TWh
- **Renewables share:** 19.8%
- **Share change:** +5.76 pp
- **Generation CAGR:** 5.44%

## Interpretation
India: renewable electricity share changed by 5.76 percentage points between 2000 and 2024. Electricity generation changed from 571.4 to 2,036.5 TWh. This descriptive comparison does not identify policy effects or investment returns.

## Research question
How did electricity generation and renewable electricity share change across six selected countries between 2000 and 2024?

## Method
The committed CSV is a checksum-verified subset of the pinned OWID dataset. The application reports observed endpoints, geometric growth in TWh and percentage-point changes in renewable share. No missing values are filled. Country selection is illustrative, not a representative global sample.

## Implication
Changes in generation scale and electricity mix are separate dimensions. A rising renewable share does not by itself imply falling absolute fossil generation. Comparisons require source coverage and policy context; this report does not estimate causal policy impacts.
